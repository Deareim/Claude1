Layer 2 connects agents and steps into a graph. After each step a condition decides where to go: forward to the next step, sideways on a branch, or back to an earlier step to try again. Facts travel along with the work, which is the shared state. In the demo, the flowchart on the right is this graph, and every hand-off pause is an edge being crossed. The next slide zooms in on the four kinds of loop-back.
