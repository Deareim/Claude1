The meta-harness is the control layer on top of everything, and in the demo it is now a real actor: the Orchestrator. It opens every case: it picks the team for the chosen loop, sets the rules, and hides private data like card numbers and emails before any agent sees them. It dispatches each agent in turn, keeps the shared case file, and counts every step against a budget. Flip the "Tight budget" switch in the control room to watch it stop a case and hand it to a person. Before any money moves, it runs the refund in a sandbox, checks the result, and only then commits it. Everything it decides lands in the audit log.
