The meta-harness is the control layer on top of everything. It decides which agent runs when, passes the work between them, and enforces rules that no single agent can override, like the stop rule: after three tries, a person takes over. It also monitors the whole run. Our demo has a simple version: the app itself. In a real company this layer manages many agent teams with sandboxes, cost limits and audit logs.
