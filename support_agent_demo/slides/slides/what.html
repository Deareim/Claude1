Agentic AI in one slide

A chatbot answers. An agent gets the job done.

Chatbot

You ask, it replies

One question, one answer. You still look up the order, decide, and send the refund yourself.

Agent

You give a goal, it works

It plans the steps, uses tools, checks the result, and asks a person before anything risky.

1 · Understand

What is the goal?

2 · Act

Use a tool or data

3 · Check

Done? If not, loop back

4 · Human gate

A person approves big calls

Open with the contrast. A chatbot is like asking a colleague a question: you get an answer, then you do the work. An agent is like handing the colleague the whole task: it figures out the steps, uses the systems it has access to, checks its own result, and comes back to you only when a decision is above its pay grade. The bottom row is the loop every agent runs: understand, act, check, and repeat until done. The orange box is the safety net: fixed rules decide when a human must approve.
