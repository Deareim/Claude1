The harness is everything around the model that makes it useful and safe. Tools let the agent act, here the order database. Context gives it the facts it needs, here the order details and the shop policy. Permissions limit what it can do alone, here the 100-euro rule. Traces record what happened, here the timeline with every step and its timing. In the app, database calls and the safety rule light up layer 3 in green.
