This is the loop people care about most: the human stays in charge. When the amount is over 100 euros, the manager reviews the proposal. A plain "no" would just end the case, which is a branch. Here the manager's comment, like "offer a 20% voucher instead", goes back to the Resolution Agent, which decides again and goes through the safety check again. If the new amount is under 100 euros it passes on its own. Three rejections trigger the stop rule and a senior specialist takes over. In the demo, pick loop 3, use the laptop example, and reject with a comment yourself, or use the switch so the manager rejects automatically.
