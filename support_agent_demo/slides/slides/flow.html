Dashed line = the loop (only when information is missing). In real use the whole trip takes seconds; the demo pauses at each hand-off.

Read it top to bottom: time flows down. The customer types a question into the app, the app starts the agent team, and the agents call out to two things: Claude for thinking, and the order database for facts. Step 5 is the loop: only when the order can't be found does the agent go back to the customer. Step 7 is the safety gate: above 100 euros the agents stop and a manager decides. Under 100 euros, steps 7 and 8 are skipped and the answer goes straight back. In the live demo, the right-hand column shows exactly these steps with real timings.
