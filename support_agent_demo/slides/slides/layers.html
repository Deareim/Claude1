The big picture

4 layers of an agent system

1 · Loop

One agent understands, reasons, acts and checks, until its goal is reached.

In the demo: each agent card, e.g. Triage reasoning with Claude.

2 · Graph

Steps joined by conditions, branches and loop-backs, sharing what they know.

In the demo: the flowchart. Diamonds decide, dashed arrows go back.

3 · Harness

What agents can use: tools, context, permissions and traces.

In the demo: order database, shop policy, the €100 rule, the timeline.

4 · Meta-harness

Runs the whole team and enforces policy, safety and monitoring.

In the demo: the Orchestrator: plan, guardrails, budget, sandbox, audit.

This is the map for the rest of the talk. Each layer builds on the one below. Layer 1 is a single agent running its own small loop. Layer 2 connects several steps into a graph with conditions and loop-backs. Layer 3, the harness, is what the system gives the agents: tools, data, permissions and logs. Layer 4, the meta-harness, sits on top: it orchestrates the agents and enforces the rules. Our support demo touches all four, and the layer strip in the app lights up whichever layer is working.
